04

Derivatives

Definition · Rules · Chain Rule · Implicit Differentiation · Higher Order · Parametric · Tangents & Normals

Key Concepts

  • Definition: $f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$
  • Power, product, quotient rules
  • Chain rule: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du}\cdot\frac{du}{dx}$
  • Derivatives of trig, exponential, log, inverse trig
  • Implicit differentiation
  • Linearization: $L(x) = f(a) + f'(a)(x-a)$
  • Parametric derivatives: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$
  • Tangent and normal lines
Stewart, Calculus §3.1–3.6, §3.8, §3.9

08

Definite Integrals

Riemann Sums · Fundamental Theorem of Calculus · Substitution in Definite Integrals · Improper Integrals

Key Concepts

  • Riemann sum: $\sum_{i=1}^n f(c_i)\Delta x$
  • Definite integral: $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = \lim_{n \to \infty} \sum f(c_i)\Delta x$
  • Sigma notation and summation formulas
  • FTC Part 1: $F(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\,dt \Rightarrow F'(x) = f(x)$
  • FTC Part 2: $\int_a^b f = F(b) - F(a)$
  • Substitution in definite integrals: change limits
  • Type I improper integrals (infinite intervals)
  • Type II improper integrals (discontinuities)
  • p-integral test; comparison test
Stewart, Calculus §5.1–5.3, §5.5, §7.8

09

Applications of Integrals

Area Between Curves · Volumes (Disk/Washer/Shells) · Arc Length · Surface Area

Key Concepts

  • Area between curves: $\int_a^b [f(x) - g(x)]\,dx$
  • Disk method: $V = \pi \int_a^b [f(x)]^2\,dx$
  • Washer method: $V = \pi \int_a^b ([f]^2 - [g]^2)\,dx$
  • Cylindrical shells: $V = 2\pi \int_a^b x f(x)\,dx$
  • Arc length: $L = \int_a^b \sqrt{1 + [f'(x)]^2}\,dx$
  • Surface area of revolution: $S = \int 2\pi r\,ds$
Stewart, Calculus §6.1, §6.2, §6.3, §8.1, §8.2
